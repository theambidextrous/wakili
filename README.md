# 🇰🇪 Wakili-AI: Kenyan Legal Intelligence Agent

Wakili-AI is a high-performance, specialized AI agent built to provide instant, accurate, and context-aware legal assistance based on the **Laws of Kenya**. From the streets of Nairobi to the Kadhis' courts, Wakili-AI bridges the gap between complex legislation and everyday legal needs.

---

## ⚖️ Introduction
In a country with a rich but complex legal framework—ranging from the **2010 Constitution** to specialized **Sharia-compliant** personal laws—accessing quick and reliable legal information is often a hurdle for many.

Wakili-AI is designed to serve as a first-line legal co-pilot. It processes the vast "Blue Books," county-specific bylaws, and recent judicial precedents to provide users with clear, actionable insights. Whether you are a legal practitioner looking for a case citation or a citizen trying to understand a traffic fine, Wakili-AI is your 24/7 legal companion.

---

## 🛠️ Core Use Cases

### 1. Constitutional & Civil Rights 📜
* **Article Interpretation:** Instant breakdown of the Bill of Rights.
* **Devolution:** Guidance on petitioning the government or understanding how power is shared under the 47 counties.

### 2. Criminal Law & Penal Codes 🚔
* **Offense Classification:** Detailed analysis of the **Penal Code (Cap 63)** and **Criminal Procedure Code**.
* **Rights of the Accused:** Real-time advice on police procedures, bail/bond terms, and "know your rights" during arrests.

### 3. Traffic & County Regulations 🚗
* **NTSA Compliance:** Instant lookup of traffic offenses and official fines to prevent roadside extortion.
* **County Bylaws:** Navigating business permits, parking fees, and noise regulations in counties like Nairobi, Mombasa, or Kisumu.

### 4. Family & Personal Law (Pluralist Systems) 💍
* **Marriage Act 2014:** Guidance on Christian, Civil, Customary, and Hindu marriages.
* **Islamic Law (Sharia):** Specialized module for **Kadhis' Court** matters, including Nikah, Talaq, and Mahr, aligned with Kenyan Sharia principles.
* **Succession:** Simplified guidance on the Law of Succession Act for inheritance and probate matters.

### 5. Corporate & Land Law 🏢
* **Business Registration:** Steps for e-Citizen company registration and KRA compliance.
* **Land Act 2012:** Checking procedures for land transfers, searches, and stamp duty.

## 🚀 Key Benefits

* **⏱️ 24/7 Accessibility:** No more waiting for office hours. Get legal clarity at midnight or on a public holiday.
* **💰 Cost-Effective:** Drastically reduces the "consultation fee" barrier for basic legal research and document drafting.
* **📚 Hyper-Local Knowledge:** Unlike general AI, Wakili-AI knows the difference between a Nairobi City County ordinance and a Kiambu County regulation.
* **✍️ Document Drafting:** Generates drafts for demand letters, tenancy agreements, and affidavits in seconds.
* **🎯 Accuracy:** Trained specifically on the **Kenya Law Reports (eKLR)** and the latest Gazetted updates.
